Rules

2026 season

1. Pick one Captain card each week before Thursday Night Football kickoff.

2. Higher PPR score wins the matchup and steals one ticket.

3. Miss the TNF deadline and you forfeit the Card Captain matchup. If your opponent submitted on time, they win even if their Captain scores 0.0. If both managers miss the deadline, there is no winner and no ticket movement.

4. Autograph cards are Defense cards: purely protective — no ticket moves in a matchup where Defense is played.

5. Dual cards combine both players’ PPR scores.

6. Each individual physical card can be played only once per league season, regardless of trades.

7. Zero tickets does not eliminate you; you can keep playing and steal your way back. Ticket balances never go below zero.

8. Zero tickets at prize time means no prize eligibility. No tickey-no prizey.
